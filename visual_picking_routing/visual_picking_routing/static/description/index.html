🚨 The Problem This Solves

Warehouse picking errors cost money. This module attacks the root causes directly.

❌ Without Visual Picking Routing

  • Operators carry a printed list sorted by product name — not by shelf location
  • They zigzag across the warehouse instead of walking a straight path
  • No product images — they must memorize or guess what each item looks like
  • Barcodes on the printed slip are tiny — scanners miss them, operators re-scan repeatedly
  • Wrong item picked = return, repack, reship = wasted cost
  • New operators are slow and error-prone with no visual reference

✔ With Visual Picking Routing

  • Picking list lines are automatically sorted by source location (A1 → A2 → B1...)
  • Operators walk a single efficient path through the warehouse
  • Each product line shows a thumbnail image so operators see exactly what to pick
  • Barcodes are enlarged on the printed document for reliable first-scan success
  • Any operator — trained or new — can pick accurately on day one
  • No changes to Odoo core — install, configure, and go

Three automatic improvements activate the moment you print a picking slip.

1

📍 Automatic Route Sorting

When you validate or print a picking operation, the module reads the source location of each move line (e.g., WH/Stock/Shelf A, WH/Stock/Shelf B) and reorders the printed lines from closest to farthest. Your operator starts at aisle A, finishes at aisle Z — no backtracking.

📈 Business impact: A 10-item pick that zigzags across 6 aisles becomes a clean 1-direction walk. Over hundreds of picks per day, this saves hours of walking time per week.
2

📷 Product Images on the Picking Slip

Each line on the printed picking slip now shows a product thumbnail image directly next to the product name. Operators confirm visually before picking — no more confusing similar-looking SKUs. Images are pulled directly from the Odoo product record, so no extra configuration is needed.

✋ Reduces wrong picks by giving operators a visual checkpoint before they grab the item. Critical for businesses with similar products (sizes, colors, variants).
3

▩ Enlarged Barcodes

The native Odoo picking report prints barcodes at a very small size, often causing scanner failures and repeated scans. This module enlarges the barcode on the printed document to a size that modern handheld scanners can read reliably, even under poor warehouse lighting conditions.

🔌 Eliminates the frustration of failed barcode scans that slow down the entire packing line. One scan, done.
