Visual Packing Slip

Zero Mistakes. 100% Visual Verification.

Empower your packing operators by injecting crystal-clear product images directly into your Odoo Delivery Slips (Packing Slips).

Why Visual Packing?

Text-based delivery slips cause mis-packs. When shipping hundreds of orders, part numbers blur together. By adding the product image directly to the PDF, packers get instant visual confirmation before sealing the box. Say goodbye to costly returns and angry customers.

Native Settings Panel

You have full control. Navigate to Inventory > Configuration > Settings to toggle images on or off instantly. You can also customize the image size (Small, Medium, Large) to save on printer ink without touching a single line of code.

Premium B2B Architecture

  • Non-Destructive: Built strictly with Odoo XPaths. Uninstalls cleanly.
  • Multi-Table Support: Works flawlessly with Serial Numbers and Lots tables.
  • Multi-Company: Settings apply per company in multi-company environments.
