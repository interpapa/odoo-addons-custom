🚨 The Problem This Solves

A mis-packed order costs far more than the price of this module. Here is how it happens — and how we stop it.

❌ Without Visual Packing Slip

  • The default Odoo delivery slip shows only product names and quantities
  • Your packing team must know every product by name — from memory
  • Similar SKUs (same product, different color or size) are impossible to distinguish at a glance
  • New packers are slow and unreliable until they memorize the catalog
  • A wrong item shipped = customer complaint, return, refund, and reshipment cost
  • During peak season with temporary staff, error rates skyrocket

✔ With Visual Packing Slip

  • Each product line on the delivery slip shows a large, clear product image
  • Packers verify visually before placing each item in the box
  • Similar SKUs are immediately distinguishable — red vs blue, S vs XL
  • Any packer — experienced or brand new — packs correctly on day one
  • No wrong items shipped = no returns, no complaints, no reshipment cost
  • Works automatically on every delivery order — no extra steps for your team

🌏 How It Works

One install. Zero workflow changes. The images appear automatically on every delivery slip.

1

📦 Create a Delivery Order as Normal

Your team processes sales orders and validates deliveries exactly as they always have. There is no change to the Odoo workflow. No new buttons, no extra steps, no training needed.

🛠️ Zero friction: The module is completely transparent to your existing process. It only activates when the delivery slip is printed.
2

📷 Print the Delivery Slip — Images Appear Automatically

When you click Print → Delivery Slip on any transfer, the generated PDF now includes a product image column next to each line item. Images are pulled directly from the product record in Odoo. If a product has no image, the line renders normally with no errors.

💬 "Is this the right item?" becomes a question your packers answer instantly — by looking at the slip, not by searching the catalog or asking a supervisor.
3

✅ Packer Verifies Visually → Packs → Ships

Your packer scans the slip: image matches the item in hand → pack it. Image does not match → they stop and correct before the box is sealed. This single visual checkpoint eliminates the most common cause of shipping errors.

📈 ROI: One prevented mis-shipment per week saves more than the annual cost of this module. For high-volume shippers, the savings are immediate and ongoing.
